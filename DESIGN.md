# Design System: VEDRIX AUTO

## Visual Theme
Bright precision engineering: confident, clean, and commercial without feeling sterile. Sections alternate through calibrated light color fields so the page has rhythm while remaining airy and professional.

## Color Roles
- **Porcelain Canvas** `#FCFDFE` — neutral page canvas.
- **Ice Engineering Blue** `#EAF3FF` — EV and business section field.
- **Silver Workshop** `#F1F4F7` — company and contact section field.
- **Soft Signal Coral** `#FFF0EF` — automobile and vision section field.
- **VEDRIX Cobalt** `#316BCB` — brand, primary controls, active states.
- **Signal Red** `#D9343A` — restrained product and mobility emphasis only.
- **Technical Ink** `#253858` — all primary text; never used as a large background.
- **Steel Copy** `#61748F` — descriptions and metadata.

## Typography
- **Display and Body:** Outfit. Tight, controlled display tracking; relaxed body leading.
- Body copy stays below 65 characters per line where practical.
- No oversized generic marketing copy and no serif typography.

## Components
- Buttons use cobalt fill, 4px corners, subtle blue-tinted shadow, and tactile translate on press.
- Cards use white surfaces only where elevation communicates a product grouping.
- Borders use blue-gray at low contrast; shadows remain soft and tinted.
- Red appears in rules, icons, and concise labels—not full UI surfaces.

## Layout
- Asymmetric left-aligned hero with clean image separation.
- Alternating section colors create progression and prevent a white-on-white page.
- Product layouts vary between bordered matrix and editorial product grid.
- Mobile collapses to one column with 44px minimum controls and no horizontal overflow.

## Banned Patterns
- No dark section backgrounds, black surfaces, neon glows, gradient text, excessive pills, generic three-card rows, fake statistics, or decorative scroll prompts.
